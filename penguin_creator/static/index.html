What am I looking at?
  • Body size follows body mass, height and flippers follow flipper length.
  • Bill length and thickness follow culmen length and depth.
  • Eggs: two for a full clutch, one if not.
  • Food: krill for low δ15N, fish for high δ15N (trophic level).
  • Water: deep blue offshore (low δ13C), teal inshore (high δ13C).
  • Backdrop: Biscoe, Dream or Torgersen island.
